Machine-learning forecast of the next EIA storage report Loading

Forecast of the weekly change in Lower 48 working gas, Bcf (positive = injection), refreshed every day.

How it works: gradient boosting and ridge regression, averaged, trained on EIA weekly storage since 2015 with population-weighted heating and cooling degree days for ten large metro areas (Open-Meteo), the five-year average for the week, last week's change and the storage surplus or deficit. Accuracy comes from a walk-forward backtest that refits every week using only data available at the time; it uses observed weather, so live errors can be a little larger when the week's last days are still a weather forecast. The chart shows the last 26 backtest weeks.
