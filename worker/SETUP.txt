NATURAL GAS PRICE RELAY (Cloudflare Worker)

The front page shows WTI and Brent from TradingView. The natural gas tile
(NYMEX NG1!) comes from Yahoo Finance through this small relay, because
TradingView does not allow NG1! in free website widgets.

Your relay is already set up at:
    https://sanjo-prices.joseph-george-2cb.workers.dev/

TO UPDATE THE RELAY CODE
1. Open worker-code.txt (this folder) in Notepad and copy everything.
   Don't double-click worker.js: Windows tries to run it and shows an error.
   It's the same code.
2. Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com > Workers & Pages > sanjo-prices.
3. Click "Edit code", replace everything with what you copied, click Deploy.
4. Check it: open the address above with ?debug=1 on the end. Under "ng1"
   you should see NYMEX's front-month symbol (e.g. NGX26.NYM) and a price.

The relay picks the same contract as TradingView's NG1! and moves to the
next month when each contract expires. index.html already points to the
address above; this folder does not need to be uploaded with the website.

Yahoo Finance quotes can run a few minutes behind the exchange.

NATURAL GAS DASHBOARD: AUTOMATIC UPDATES
gas_dashboard.html now pulls live data from this relay every time it opens.
If anything can't load, that part of the page keeps the built-in Oct 26 numbers.

Updates by itself:
  - EIA weekly storage report: total, regions, vs year ago and 5-year average
    (every Thursday after 9:30 AM CT; works even without a key)
  - Henry Hub futures, front month (delayed quote; works without a key)
  - Storage history chart and 4-week projection        (needs EIA key)
  - Henry Hub spot: weekly chart and month-to-date     (needs EIA key)
  - EIA STEO: production, demand, LNG exports, balance,
    Henry Hub forecasts, end-of-month storage forecast (needs EIA key)
  - US LNG exports by destination country, monthly     (needs EIA key)
  - EU gas storage % full vs 5-year average            (needs GIE key)
Still updated by hand (no free source): rig counts, AGA year-to-date numbers,
regional basis, JKM, LNG feedgas, infrastructure events, tanker map.
TTF has been removed from the dashboard.

SETUP (one time, about 15 minutes)
1. Update the relay code: follow "TO UPDATE THE RELAY CODE" above.
2. EIA key (free): https://www.eia.gov/opendata/register.php
   Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > sanjo-prices > Settings >
   Variables and Secrets > Add. Type: Secret. Name: EIA_API_KEY. Value: your key.
3. GIE key for EU storage (free): register at https://agsi.gie.eu (top right),
   then copy your API key from your account page. Add it the same way, with
   the name AGSI_API_KEY.
4. Deploy, then upload gas_dashboard.html to Netlify with the rest of the site.
5. Check it: open the relay address with ?dashboard=1&debug=1 on the end.
   Each section (storage_report, steo, henry_hub, ...) should show data.
   Anything that failed is listed under "errors" with the reason; send me a
   screenshot of that page and I'll fix it.
The dashboard's top-left chip says "Live · updated <time>" when live data
loaded, or "Snapshot" if it didn't.

JKM (ASIA LNG) ON THE HOME PAGE
Yahoo doesn't carry JKM, so the relay uses OilPriceAPI (free plan, 50 requests
a day; the relay asks at most once an hour).
1. Sign up free at https://www.oilpriceapi.com and copy your API key.
2. Cloudflare > sanjo-prices > Settings > Variables and Secrets > Add.
   Type: Secret. Name: OILPRICE_API_KEY. Value: your key. Deploy.
3. Open the relay address with ?debug=1: "jkm" should show a price.
Without the key, the JKM tile shows the last price you entered in index.html
(GAS_BACKUP), labeled "Not live" with its date.

WEEKLY EIA INVENTORY REPORTS (crude, gasoline, distillate, natural gas storage)
1. Get a free EIA API key: https://www.eia.gov/opendata/register.php
   (enter your name and email; the key arrives by email).
2. In Cloudflare: Workers & Pages > sanjo-prices > Settings >
   Variables and Secrets > Add. Type: Secret. Name: EIA_API_KEY.
   Value: your key. Save and deploy.
3. Open the worker address with ?debug=1. Under "reports" you should see
   four headlines such as "USA Crude Oil Inventories 1.892M Barrel Draw".
The home page shows the strip only when the reports load, so nothing
appears until the key is added. Analyst estimates are not included
(they come from paid services).
